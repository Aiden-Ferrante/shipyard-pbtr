# v0.29.2 — The draft queue

Light cycle plan under [[0.) Development Protocol 1.0]]
Opened 2026-08-26

Addresses **An idea is a candidate sub-problem, or it is a note**

> Every item that left the queue left by gaining a waypoint; over a month, nothing
> was worked on that entered any other way.

## Problem

The arc's plan called for a todo list the Yard picks work from. Two mechanisms
already existed and neither was recognised as one:

- `create_problem(draft=True)` — the fast-capture opt-out from the waypoint
  requirement, on the stated grounds that *"you cannot demand an observable between
  pressing Enter and having the next thought"*. A draft is flagged, counted as debt
  on the goals page, and refused a repo link. What it never had was a surface that
  asks you to finish it.
- `work.py`'s `backlog/tasks/*.md` orphans, which need the `backlog` CLI on PATH and
  a `backlog/` directory most of the fleet does not have.

Building a third would have been the mistake. The queue is drafts.

## Appetite

One sitting. A read, a band, and the round-trip test.

## Plan

`_queue(con)` composed in `main.py` from `chart.outline`, joined into `/api/yard`
additively — bays say what blocks entry, the ledger says what is moving, the queue
says what is not yet startable. A `QueueBand` at the top of the Yard and a queue
stage on the map, ahead of the gate.

**Non-goals.** No promote verb: promotion *is* writing the waypoint. No second
store. Not `backlog/tasks` — joining two stores behind one surface is the divergence
this codebase avoids everywhere else.

## Exit criteria

A draft appears in the queue; writing its waypoint removes it *and* makes it
cycle-openable. Both transitions tested. `make test` and `make check` green.

## Build

**The band was useless on first render, and only the real chart showed it.** Run
against the live store before any filter, the queue opened with `<root goal>`
and its five `<domain>` groupings — six of twelve rows
were the skeleton the whole chart hangs from, waypointless because a grouping has no
observable of its own and never will. A band whose entire question is *what may I
start next* would have opened with six things that can never be started.

The filter is `chart.children()` — the same discriminator `_open_gate` settled on in
v0.29.1, arrived at again from the other direction. A node with problems beneath it
composes work rather than being work. Its draft debt is still counted on the goals
page, which is where a question about the chart's *shape* belongs.

Six real rows remain, and every one of them is a genuine unfinished thought.

**No promote button, and that is the design.** The moment a draft has a waypoint it
stops being a draft, `_open_gate` stops refusing it, and a cycle can be cut against
it. So the row links to the problem and the chart's own editor does the rest. A
second verb here would be a second way to do the one thing, which is how two
surfaces come to disagree.

References are excluded, matching `/api/goals`: `outline` emits a shared node once
expandable and once inert, and a queue listing both would ask you to finish it twice.

## Test

`make test` — **441 backend + 5 frontend, green** (438 before this cycle, three added here). `make check` clean.

**Both filters have negative tests, run rather than assumed.** Removing the grouping
filter fails `test_a_grouping_is_not_in_the_queue`; removing the draft filter fails
that one *and* the round-trip. Restored, all nineteen in `test_yard.py` pass.

`test_shape_is_exactly_the_contract` needed widening for the new key — which is
exactly what that test is for, and it caught the addition on the first run.

**Driven in the browser**, on a second instance at :8847 so the running one was left
alone. The map reads `THE QUEUE · 6 · captured, needs a waypoint`; the band lists the
six oldest-first with what each serves and how long it has waited; no console errors.
Clicking the first row lands on `#/problem/c591725af973`, which says *"No waypoint
yet — nothing here can be attacked. Write one →"*. The promotion path is coherent end
to end, and the copy that explains it was already written.

## Review

**The cycle that was mostly deletion of my own plan.** Two of the three things the
goal prompt implied were needed — a store and a promote verb — turned out to exist or
to be unnecessary. What shipped is a read, a band, and one filter.

**The filter is the whole cycle.** Everything else was wiring; the band would have
been ignored within a week with the bearings at the top of it, and no test would have
caught that because every row was legitimately a waypointless problem. It was found
by running `_queue` against the real store and reading the six titles — the
render-the-real-artifact discipline paying out inside a single sitting.

**`chart.children()` has now been the right answer twice**, in two cycles, for two
different questions: which cycles contend for the one-open slot, and which drafts are
candidates. That is a real seam — *composes work* versus *is work* — and the ADR owed
for D2/D3 should name it rather than describe it twice.

**What carries forward.** The falsifier for this leaf is that the queue fills and
never drains, and nothing here can tell yet: six items entered by existing, and none
has left. That is a month's observation, not a cycle's.
